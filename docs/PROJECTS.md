YangSlots, YangDocs, YangQueue, YangData, YangHooks and YangLab were extracted from DTDD branches into separate repositories. Their source notices and [migration catalog](https://github.com/nguyenhonggiang2407/DTDD/blob/main/docs/branch-catalog.md) document the original branches, preserved history and reference sources. A new repository does not imply a project was built entirely from scratch.
